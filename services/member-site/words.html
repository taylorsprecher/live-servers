Words Generator

Draft words from your own notes — a Python tool that writes in your vocabulary and phrasing, toward the 95,000-word goal (433 in as of Nov 22, 2026).

What it does

Point the tool at your note files and it trains on them, then drafts new text at whatever length you ask for. Every word it uses comes from your notes — it recombines your own phrases into fresh draft paragraphs, and stamps the output as machine-drafted raw material.

Python stdlib onlySelftest passed

  • python3 words_generator.py notes.md --words 500 --out draft.md
  • --seed "a phrase from the notes" starts the draft from your phrase.
  • --random-seed 42 makes a run repeatable.
  • It refuses thin material — under 200 words of notes, it asks for more instead of guessing.

The honest limit

It is a drafting spur, not a ghostwriter. The sentences come out in your voice, but it can splice facts mid-thought, so everything it makes needs your edit before it counts toward the 95,000. The more of your own notes for the book it trains on, the more it sounds like you.
