The three radios

Internet Checking

Pinging your member server every 15 seconds. This is the live proof that one of the radios below is carrying you.

Cellular Not tested

A web page can’t read the cellular radio directly — so test it for real:

  1. Tap Start cellular test below.
  2. Swipe down for Control Center and turn Wi-Fi off.
  3. Stay on this page for 30 seconds. If the pings keep landing, cellular data is carrying you.
  4. Turn Wi-Fi back on when you’re done.

Wi-Fi Not tested

The mirror test, for when you’re home:

  1. Tap Start Wi-Fi test below.
  2. Open Settings → Cellular and turn Cellular Data off.
  3. Stay on this page for 30 seconds. If the pings keep landing, your Wi-Fi is carrying you.
  4. Turn Cellular Data back on when you’re done.

Bluetooth Check by eye

iPhone web pages can’t read the Bluetooth radio at all — Apple doesn’t allow it. The 5-second check: swipe down for Control Center; the Bluetooth symbol is blue when on, grey when off. Bluetooth is what links your iPad (hotspot sharing), headsets, and the car.

Keep it open

Stay-awake watch Off

Keeps the screen on so the watch and the timeline keep running — handy on a Flex block or while tethering the iPad.

Honest limit: iOS pauses every web app when you switch away or lock the phone. The timeline resumes and re-checks the moment you come back, and nothing here can hold a radio on if iOS itself turns it off.
