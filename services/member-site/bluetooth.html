Bluetooth Check-in

The tool for the sealed Bluetooth check-in log: append an entry, re-seal it, and decode the sealed copy — all under the dedicated encryption key created for it on Nov 26, 2026.

Download

The bundle holds the tool and its README. The encryption key is not in the bundle and is recorded nowhere — it stays in Taylor’s files, mode 600. Without the key, nothing seals or decodes, by design.

Download bluetooth-checkin.zip

What it does

bluetooth_checkin.py add "text of the entry"
bluetooth_checkin.py show
bluetooth_checkin.py verify

add appends a timestamped entry (Pacific), re-seals the encrypted copy, and verifies the round-trip. show decodes the sealed copy with the key and prints the log. verify checks the sealed copy decodes to exactly the plaintext on file.

Honest scope

The key seals and decodes our records about Bluetooth — the check-in statements, logged verbatim. It does not decode live Bluetooth radio traffic: the devices’ own pairing keys never leave the devices.

Built Nov 26, 2026. The sealed log and its key live in Taylor’s files; only the tool is published here.
