Shared library — PETG tolerances

lib/petg_tolerances.scad carries the petg_hole module and the standard clearance parameters used across the set (petg_hole_comp = 0.35, fit_snug = 0.25, keystone_clearance = 0.20). Offsets summary: holes +0.35 mm, slot −0.25 mm, keystone +0.20 mm/side — accounting for PETG shrinkage and perimeter squish on snap-fit tabs, mounting bosses, and keystone latches.

Toyota Corolla Hatchback (E210) — Dash RJ45 LAN Housings

Tag: TOYOTA

Single-port, dual-port, and console flange-mount keystone housings for Toyota dash switch blanks (22.5 × 22.5 mm bezel, 18.2 × 18.2 mm snap-fit body, 14.8 × 16.2 mm keystone cutout; dual-port 32.5 × 22 mm switch blank variant), plus single- and dual-port round threaded bulkhead variants with D-flat anti-rotation — the dual adds a parametric center reinforcement rib. Print profile: PETG or ABS/ASA, 0.16–0.20 mm layers, 30–50% infill, face-down. Source: AppleWatch LAN.docx.

Boeing — Aerospace Panel-Mount LAN Connector Housing

MIL-DTL-38999-inspired circular flange (38 mm outer diameter, 31 mm 4-hole bolt circle) with a 28 mm barrel and a 16.5 × 16.5 mm pass-through cavity for shielded CAT6A or Quadrax inserts. The bundle carries the “Fully Featured” variant; five variants exist in the source. Source: BOEING 3D LAN.docx.

iPhone Air & Prototype Chassis Modules

Ultra-thin chassis concept (156.2 × 74.7 mm, 12 mm corner radius, 0.8 mm Minkowski edge bevels; depth fixed to 5.6 mm) plus a 50 × 50 × 18 mm LAN test dock with a 5.8 mm device slot (+0.2 mm tolerance), RJ45 jack cutout, and USB-C routing trench. Sources: iPhone Air.docx, iPhone LAN connection set-up.docx.

Apple TV Air — Drop-Jaw LAN Shell

84 × 28 × 6.8 mm rounded enclosure with a rear spring-loaded expansion slot for Cat6 insertion. Source: AppleTV Air LAN.docx.
