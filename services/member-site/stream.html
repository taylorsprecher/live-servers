Stream Mic

Streams this device's microphone — or its screen plus microphone — to your own member server in 10-second chunks. Only you, signed in as the owner, can open this page or receive a stream. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Stopped.

Keep this page open and do not close it while streaming. Each chunk is saved on the server with a timestamp; ask Jasmine for the audio, video or a transcript of any session.

The same receiver also runs standalone: stream_receiver.py (Python, no dependencies).
